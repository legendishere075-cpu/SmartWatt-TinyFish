ENGINEERING METHODOLOGY

Simple, auditable calculations.

E = P × t × n × d / 1000

Where E is energy (kWh), P is rated power (W), t is hours per day, n is quantity, and d is days.

Estimated cost = E × tariff

This is an estimate based on rated power and assumed operating time. Real consumption varies with duty cycle, efficiency, and operating conditions.
